ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm María — a travel content creator passionate about authentic experiences and helping brands connect with their audience through engaging UGC. I specialize in natural, relatable content that highlights the true essence of a place — from hidden gems and local culture to the magic of everyday adventures.

With a strong eye for photography and video, I create high-quality content that showcases your destination as the top choice in your area. My goal is to inspire people to explore while helping your brand stand out. Let's work together and make something great.

WHY UGC?

User-Generated Content (UGC) builds trust, drives engagement, and captures attention like no other format. In travel, people seek real experiences — not staged promotions. UGC shows your destination through the eyes of real travelers, making it easier for potential visitors to connect emotionally with your brand and take action.

MY RECORDING DEVICES

I shoot most of my content with my iPhone 17 Pro Max, which allows me to create high-quality visuals quickly and naturally, keeping the content relatable while still professional.

For travel, landscapes, and accommodation features, I love adding drone footage to give brands that cinematic, scroll-stopping perspective.

My Nikon digital camera helps me create retro-style photos with personality and a nostalgic vibe that audiences connect with.

When I need dynamic, action-driven shots, I use my DJI Osmo Action 5 Pro to capture sharp, stable footage even on the move.

For more polished and cinematic storytelling, I film with the DJI Osmo Pocket 3, achieving smooth motion and a more elevated production feel.

To make sure the sound quality matches the visuals, I use DJI Mic Mini wireless microphones for clear, crisp audio in voiceovers, interviews, and real-life scenes.

I also create alongside my partner, Álvaro, who helps me capture creative angles and behind-the-scenes moments that make each project feel natural and engaging.
